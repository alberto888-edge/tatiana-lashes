Questions

Before you come

How do I book?

WhatsApp or call 695 47 58 92, or book on Treatwell. If you pick services in the price list, the WhatsApp message writes itself.

How much is a gel polish manicure?

Express gel polish (no cuticle work) is €13, the long-lasting gel manicure with cuticle care is €20 and the rubber base manicure is €25.

Do you do acrylic and gel nails?

Yes. New acrylic sets from €37 (with manicure and gel polish, price depends on length), acrylic infill €32 and gel infill €35.

What lash extensions do you offer?

Classic (€50), 2D and 3D (€55) and 4D and 5D (€60). Infills from €30, with a surcharge after 4 weeks. Lash lifts from €25.

Where are you?

Calle de Hernani 25 (Tetuán, 28020 Madrid), about a 5-minute walk from Cuatro Caminos metro.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday 10:00–20:00 and Saturday 10:00–14:00. Closed on Sunday.
